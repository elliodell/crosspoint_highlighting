# Almanac: Reading Stats

Reading time, streaks and progress for every book, on your reader and on the web at www.almanac.im. No account, nothing to do while reading.

1. Open Home > Plugins > Almanac: Reading Stats and choose Sign in.
2. Scan the QR code with your phone and confirm the code.

Stats are sent when the reader goes to sleep or when you open the plugin. Fetch on any row puts them on the sleep screen, in place of your own pictures (Settings > Display > Sleep Screen > Custom).

Only file names, reading time and progress leave the reader: www.almanac.im/privacy
